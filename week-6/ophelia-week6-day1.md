## How to evaluate Performance

### Types of Metrics

1. Model-Centric or Technical Metrics "Loss" - closely aligned with what is being done with the Model. (e.g. taking parameter and trying again).
2. Business Centric or Outcome Metrics - Actual Difference in Price - The Metrics are mostly closely aligned with the actual Commercial with actual objectives.

## Data is typically divided into 3 datasets

- Training Data: - The Majority of Data used to learn pattern to make new predictions.
- Validation Data: (Test Data) - Held out data used to evaluate whether the Model can make new predictions accurately.
- Test Data: - Held out data used at the end of the process to confirm model prediction accuracy.
